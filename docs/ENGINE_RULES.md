# Engine Rules

1. Accept PDF or DXF.
2. DXF may contain multiple drawings.
3. Always split DXF first.
4. Process each drawing independently.
5. Do not dimension legends, tables, title blocks, frames, or notes.
6. Extract normalized line registry first.
7. Group locally and semantically.
8. Do not dimension every raw line.
9. Use perimeter-first strategy.
10. Use dimension ownership and architectural importance.
11. Export per drawing: split DXF, dimensioned DXF, PNG, PDF, report.
